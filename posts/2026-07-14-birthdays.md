In second grade, I had to report on my family tree.\
So I asked my dad when his birthday was and he\
replied, “everyday I get to eat is my birthday.”\
Back then I was frustrated but now,\
on every birthday,\
I find myself thinking,\
“I woke up today. I can walk. I have food and water.\
I have people that care for me.\
Ahh yes, it must be my birthday.”
